Sparklift

Movement and art find everyone, if we bring it to them.

Sparklift brings dance and art to people who don't usually get access to it, in hospitals, nursing homes, and preschools, and gives students anywhere a real way to help, no matter how far away they are.

What we do

One mission, three ways to show up.

Whether you're across town or across the country, there's a real role for you in bringing movement and art to people who need it most.

🎨

In-person sessions

Weekly dance and art sessions at hospitals, nursing homes, and preschools, led by trained student volunteers using our session guides.

🌍

Local chapters

Student-run groups at other schools running their own sessions and events, using our starter kits and curriculum.

💻

Remote volunteering

Design resource cards, write letters, make videos, or fundraise, all from home, with hours logged and verified.
